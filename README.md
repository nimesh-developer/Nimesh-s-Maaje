# Biological Catastrophe Engine

This is a proof-of-concept design for a highly chaotic, physics-based sandbox game focusing on absurd bodily mechanics and temporal-dimensional mayhem.

## Features

### The "Biological Catastrophe" Mechanics
* **Inner-Ear Vestibular Mini-Game:** Rapid head movements unbalance the inner ear fluid, flipping the camera and causing the character to lose control.
* **Manual Pupillary Dilation:** Transitioning between light levels requires manual pupil control; failure blinds the player.
* **The "Allergy Sneeze Rocket":** Allergens prompt a frantic input mini-game. Failure results in an explosive sneeze that propels the player backward at high speeds.
* **Digestive Peristalsis QTE:** Eating requires rhythmic inputs to digest food; missing beats causes violent acid-reflux spasms.
* **Thermoregulation Shivers:** Cold weather necessitates button-mashing to shiver and generate heat, violently shaking the player.

### 4D Tesseract Physics & Spacetime Mayhem
* **The 4th Spatial Dimension (W-Axis Kicking):** Extreme ragdoll impacts clip into the 4th dimension, causing limbs to teleport across the map.
* **Temporal Ragdoll Echoes:** Unconscious players leave a 2-minute "temporal ghost" ragdoll when they wake up at the hospital.

### Sandbox & Bad-Idea Vehicle Mechanics
* **Biomechanical Vehicles:** Bicycles and go-karts require manual articulation of leg joints to operate.
* **Kid-Friendly Ragdoll Carnage:** High-speed vehicle crashes result in cartoonish, harmless stretching rather than fatal injuries.
* **Schoolyard Bully Boss Arena:** Defeating bullies requires complex manual maneuvers while managing vital signs to prevent fainting.

### LAN & Multiplayer Chaos
* **"Dual-Brain" Two-Player Co-op:** One player controls involuntary systems (heart, breathing) while the other controls voluntary muscles.
* **Physics Tug-of-War & Human Ladders:** Players can link limbs to form unstable human structures like ladders or bridges.
* **Facial Muscle Emote Wheel:** Communication requires individually tweaking facial muscles, often resulting in nightmarish expressions.
