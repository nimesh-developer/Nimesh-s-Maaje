Moving Trashcan - Assembly Instructions

This guide details the physical and electrical assembly of the moving trashcan. The primary goal is stability to prevent the trashcan from tipping over during sudden movements.

Step 1: Preparing the Base and Chassis
1. Start with the heavy base plate (metal or thick acrylic). This is the foundation.
2. Mount the 4 DC motors to the base plate using motor brackets. Ensure they are aligned for the Mecanum wheels.
3. Attach the Mecanum wheels to the motors. Make sure the rollers on the wheels form an 'X' pattern when viewed from the top to ensure correct omnidirectional movement.
4. Mount the heavy 12V LiPo battery as close to the center and as low on the base plate as possible. This acts as ballast.
5. Attach any additional weights symmetrically to the lowest point of the base. The lower the center of gravity (CG), the less likely it is to tip.

Step 2: Electrical Assembly (Low Center)
1. Mount the Motor Drivers on the base plate.
2. Wire the 4 DC motors to the motor drivers.
3. Mount the Step-Down Buck Converter (if using one to power the Pi from the 12V battery) on the base plate.
4. Connect the battery to the motor drivers and the buck converter (make sure to include a main power switch).

Step 3: Mounting the Raspberry Pi and Sensors
1. Mount the Raspberry Pi on the base plate (or slightly higher if space is constrained, but keep it low).
2. Wire the control pins from the Motor Drivers to the Raspberry Pi GPIO pins.
3. Connect the Raspberry Pi power input to the 5V source.

Step 4: Attaching the Trashcan (The Catching Area)
1. Place the empty trashcan on top of the completed base chassis.
2. Use strong L-brackets and bolts to secure the bottom of the trashcan to the base plate. It must be rigid; any flexing can lead to tipping.
3. Install cross-supports if the trashcan is tall and flimsy.

Step 5: Camera Mounting
1. Attach the sturdy camera mount to the top lip/rim of the trashcan.
2. Secure the Raspberry Pi Camera to the mount. It should point slightly upwards and forwards to track incoming trash.
3. Run the camera ribbon cable down the side of the trashcan (use tape or a cable track) and connect it to the Raspberry Pi at the base.

Step 6: Final Stability Check
1. Before powering on, push the trashcan gently from the top. It should resist tipping and naturally want to right itself due to the heavy base.
2. Ensure no wires are loose or can get caught in the Mecanum wheels.
